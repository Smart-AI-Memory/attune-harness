## Decision and analysis

The continuation floor requires all eight Luna-selected attempts and all sixteen
policy-selected attempts to pass, no preservation miss, and at least 25% fewer
worker credits than always-Sol. Report all original outcomes, including cases
sent to Sol that Luna also passed. A passing selection with frequent unnecessary
Sol calls may still be a poor classifier. Fixed policy costs select from paired
replies; they do not measure deployed routing or a failed-Luna-then-Sol cascade.

Eight related synthetic cases in four families cannot establish “definitely
passes” or population reliability. Isolated sources are 112–311 bytes; embedded
versions are 1,185–1,384 bytes. They do not reproduce the earlier 9.7 KB file's
full replacement burden. The strongest counter-case to spending is
that both models may pass everything again, offering little evidence that the
more expensive selections are needed. This design nevertheless tests preservation
surface directly and can falsify the claimed safe Luna subset on new cases.

Stop on unknown usage/dispatch, authentication ambiguity, source drift, host
enforcement failure or exhausted allowance. Retain ordinary resolved failures
without retries. A clean result earns further qualification only; no Task 8
acceptance, production router, live activation or release is included.

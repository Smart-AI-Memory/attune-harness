## D14: the five decisions in Task 3's design note

[Task 3's design note](task-3-design.md) named five decisions. At 08:15 UTC
Patrick answered "1-5: y, all as recommended."

1. **Approach.** Carry `elicitation/command_workspace.py` and
   `spec/workspace.py` from Attune AI at `b89f7953f`, adapt them at the three
   seams the note names, and make Harness's task store the authority for R1.
   Cross-process refusal of stale and replayed decisions comes from
   `work_contract.bind_work_acceptance` and `work_decisions`, under the store's
   lease, as it does today; the host's `asyncio.Lock` is an in-process
   convenience and proves nothing across processes. A smaller host written
   around the store is rejected: it would drop 22 tests and the nonce, drift
   and terminal checks to save about 300 lines.
2. **Telemetry.** The host's two events, a render and a collect, are written
   into the task directory beside `decision.json`, as evidence rather than
   telemetry. Nothing is written under `ATTUNE_HOME` or `~/.attune`.
3. **Where progress lives.** The task store is the authority. The plan's
   trailing state comment, which 2.3's `spec_state.save_state` writes, is a
   projection: written after the store, never read as authority for a
   decision. Other projects' plans are read and converted, never written, as
   D6 already rules.
4. **Which stages.** Task 3 wires the task gate only: `approve_task`,
   `redo_task`, `auto_run_remaining`, and for high severity `fix_retry` and
   `acknowledge_risk`. The spec-creation stages carried inside `workspace.py`
   stay unwired until Task 4 switches plan and build.
5. **Sequencing.** Four pull requests, each with a different-model review
   under [the standing brief](../../review-brief.md): 3.1
   `command_workspace.py`; 3.2 the four `spec_intake` names; 3.3
   `workspace.py`; 3.4 the bridge switch with the R1 and R3 tests. 3.1 and 3.2
   may be built in parallel, since they touch nothing in common. 3.3 waits for
   both to merge; 3.4 waits for 3.3. One go for the set covers the merges
   under the shepherding delegation in `AGENTS.md`; a changed assertion is
   never the tests-only exception.

Evidence Task 3 ends with, from the note: two processes against one task
directory, the second's decision refused and one acceptance in the store; a
replay after acceptance refused; every wired action refused without its

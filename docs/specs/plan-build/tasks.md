checks may run concurrently when authorized; this outline does not authorize
delegated agents, providers or a permanent multi-agent committee by itself.

does not defer safety enforcement until after build effects are implemented.

Task 8's exact native participants, sample size, acceptance floors and spend
estimate must be frozen before those trials. Existing review/fix grades cannot
substitute for plan/build outcome evidence.

Tasks 1 and 8 also establish and compare time to first useful finding/decision,
including inspection, validation, hooks, model turns and actual presentation.
Report full completion and human response time separately; renderer-only timing
cannot establish the user's observed latency.

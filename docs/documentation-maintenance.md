# Documentation content maintenance

Patrick's direction, September 18, 2026: include the opportunity log and its
associated content whenever the project receives a complete documentation review.
Review accuracy, usefulness and current status as well as spelling and links.
This is part of the documentation review, not a separate approval gate or schedule.

## Review checklist

1. **Reconcile opportunities.** Check the [current register](opportunity-log.md)
   against code, saved results and accepted decisions. Separate open work,
   completed local improvements, rollout, live qualification and deferred work.
   Update next steps and priorities; a dated historical proposal is not an active
   task. Keep identifiers unique and retain reasons for closure or deferral.
2. **Update connected summaries.** Check the [README](../README.md),
   [release journey map](release-journey-map.md),
   [readiness direction](harness-release-readiness-direction.md), capability maps
   and relevant specs. Verify present-tense claims against the implementation.
   Preserve historical reports as evidence; label their time and scope rather
   than rewriting old results to match later improvements.
3. **Check user guidance.** Review the [warning and guidance examples](user-guidance-examples.md),
   CLI/help text and decision forms together. Explain what happened, whether it
   blocks progress and the valid next action. Distinguish optional advice,
   unavailable checks, failures, uncertainty and completion. Check that durable
   text and short replies still refer to the current decision. Label proposed
   wording until the corresponding behavior exists.
4. **Check public claims.** Review the [controls article](blog/controls-savings-user-journey.md)
   and associated content for clear language, preserved intent and evidence.
   Separate measured cost/time from potential savings, replay from fresh model
   performance, and prevention of regressions from removal of existing technical
   debt. Preserve original grades and contamination disclosures. Verify numbers
   and links against the underlying evidence, not just another summary.
5. **Check publication status.** Consult the [website handoff](handoffs/smartaimemory-controls-article.md).
   Keep draft, approved and published states accurate. When a public version
   exists, review it alongside its source and check public links. Do not expose
   private receipts or conversations through publication links.
6. **Record the scope of the review.** Date the reviewed register, identify
   substantive changes and retain unresolved items with a concrete next step.
   Report broken links or unverifiable claims. A link check alone is not a
   completed content review; do not imply the whole corpus was checked after a
   narrow sweep.

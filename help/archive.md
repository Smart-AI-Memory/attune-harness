## Read the evidence in context

Each link identifies a version or preparation state. Published software checks,
synthetic maintainer walkthroughs, illustrative outputs and independent user
acceptance are different evidence. Keep Windows limitations and provider-call
boundaries with the record that states them. Nothing in this archive grants
execution, paid calls or approval of a saved request.

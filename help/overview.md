## Keep the decisions separate

Opening a form, saving answers, approving intent and authorizing execution are
separate actions. A saved or accepted request does not start a build or grant a
paid call. Each walkthrough states its expected result and what it does not
establish.

Historical 1.3.0 and candidate walkthroughs remain in the Harness repository
with their original source and artifact records. Harness owns and builds this
Help Center; its [1.5.1 release record](/harness/help/release-1.5.1.json)
distinguishes software checks from walkthrough and platform acceptance.
